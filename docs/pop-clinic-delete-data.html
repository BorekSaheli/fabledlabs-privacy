Pop Clinic: delete your data

Developer: FabledLabs · App: Pop Clinic: Satisfying Pops (com.fabledlabs.popclinic)

Pop Clinic has no accounts. What it keeps about you is on your phone, except two things that are sent only with your choice: gameplay analytics (Google Analytics for Firebase) and, if you said yes, squeeze recordings.

Squeeze recordings

  1. Open Pop Clinic and tap the gear (Settings).
  2. Tap Squeeze recordings, then Delete my recordings.

Every recording your phone sent is deleted from our database, recordings still waiting on the phone are removed, and recording stops. If you no longer have the phone, write to us (the developer e-mail address on the app's Google Play page) with the research ID shown in that screen, and we delete them. Recordings are otherwise deleted after at most 12 months.

Gameplay analytics

Turn it off in Settings > Share how you play. Analytics events carry only a random app-instance identifier, so we cannot pick out which data is yours; Google deletes event data after at most 14 months. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data removes the identifier.

On your phone

Your progress, coins, looks, settings and the age you entered stay on your phone. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data in Android's settings deletes them.
