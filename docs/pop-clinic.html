Privacy Policy — Pop Clinic: Satisfying Pops

Developer: FabledLabs · App ID: com.fabledlabs.popclinic · Last updated: November 10, 2026

Summary

  • No account or sign-up is needed.
  • The app may send gameplay analytics to FabledLabs through Google Analytics, depending on your country and age (see Analytics), and, only if you say yes, recordings of how you squeeze (see Squeeze recordings).
  • The app stores your game progress (patients treated, Career progress, coins, instruments, the looks you own and wear, the daily bonus), the items you bought, your settings and the age you entered only on your device.
  • Ads are provided by Google AdMob, which may collect device data as described below.

Data stored on your device

The app keeps your game progress (patients treated, Career progress, coins, instruments, the looks you own and wear, the daily bonus), the items you bought, your settings and the age you entered in its private storage on your device. This data never leaves your device through us. It is removed when you uninstall the app or clear its data. It may be included in your own Android device backup if you have backups enabled, which is governed by Google's terms.

Age question

When you first open the app, it asks how old you are. The answer stays on your device, in its own file, and is never sent to us. The app uses it to decide whether analytics may be used (see "Analytics" below) and to configure Google's ads and consent tools for players under 16 (see "Children" below). You are asked again if you start over as a new player.

Analytics

FabledLabs uses Google Analytics for Firebase to learn how people play: the customers they treat and how each spot went (for example how many tries it took, how long, how hard the in-game squeeze was and how close the fingers were to the spot, as summary numbers, never the finger movements themselves), the screens they visit, their in-game settings, what they look at and buy in the shop, coins earned and spent, how they respond to ads (for example a video offered, watched or skipped), and how the game runs on their phone (loading times, slow frames, the graphics chip's name, and errors such as a save that could not be written). Events include screens, tools, items, timings, ad outcomes and purchase outcomes. Google Analytics links these events with a random app-instance identifier stored on your device and processes device and app information and an approximate location (country or region). It does not receive your name, an account, the age you entered, your advertising ID or purchase tokens. FabledLabs links Google Analytics with its Google AdMob and Google Play accounts, so that ad performance, ad earnings and purchases can be seen next to how the game is played; through these links Google Analytics and AdMob share reports with each other. The app tells Google Analytics that its data may not be used for ads: no advertising storage, no ad user data and no ad personalization, so it is not used to choose or personalize the ads you see.

What happens depends on the country of your Google Play account. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland, Canada, Turkey, South Korea, China, India, Russia, Vietnam, Thailand, Indonesia and Saudi Arabia, and whenever the country is not known, the app asks once and sends nothing unless you choose Share. In other countries analytics is on for players aged 16 or older. Either way you can turn it on or off at any time in Settings > Share how you play. For players under 16, analytics is always off. Turning it off stops further collection. Google keeps event data for at most 14 months and then deletes it. Because the data carries only a random identifier and nothing that names you, we cannot pick out which data is yours; if you have a question about it, contact us at the address below.

Squeeze recordings (optional)

Players aged 18 or older are asked, once and separately, whether Pop Clinic may record how their fingers move while they treat their next 50 customers. Only after a yes, the app records, during treatment only: finger positions and timing on the skin, the touch pressure the phone reports, the tool, the spots and how each one came out, and how the game reacted (for example how hard the in-game squeeze was). Nothing from menus, the shop or text. Each customer's recording is sent to FabledLabs' Google Cloud Firestore database in the European Union (the Netherlands), with Google acting as our processor. A recording carries a random research ID made on your phone and the game version, and no name, account, device ID, advertising ID or analytics ID; it is not combined with analytics and never used to identify anyone or for ads. We use the recordings only to make squeezing feel right, and keep them at most 12 months. The legal basis is your consent. You can stop at any time in Settings > Squeeze recordings, and "Delete my recordings" there deletes every recording your phone sent. If you no longer have the phone, write to us with the research ID shown in that screen. You also have the rights of access and complaint to your data protection authority.

Vibration

The app uses your phone's vibration motor for haptic feedback. You can turn it off in Settings.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, and to prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process data such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, coarse location derived from the IP address, device model and OS version, and interactions with ads, and diagnostic and crash information about the ads SDK. This data is collected by Google, not by us, and is handled under Google's Privacy Policy (see also how Google uses data from apps that use its services).

If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before personalized ads are shown, using Google's consent tool. You can change your choice at any time from the app's Settings screen ("Ad privacy choices"). You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android device settings. The app shows a full-screen ad after some results. Any purchase, such as "Remove ads", turns those full-screen ads off. Short videos that pay in-game coins stay available, and you only see one when you choose to watch it. The app shows no banner ads.

In-app purchases

Purchases are processed by Google Play. We never see or store your payment details. The app only receives a confirmation of which items you own so it can unlock them.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Players under 16 are treated as below the age of digital consent: their ad requests are marked as child-directed and under the age of consent, so Google serves them non-personalized ads only.

Security

Data exchanged with Google's ad and billing services is encrypted in transit (HTTPS).

Changes

We may update this policy. The "last updated" date above shows the latest version.

Contact

Questions or requests: use the developer email address shown on the app's Google Play store page, or open an issue at github.com/BorekSaheli/fabledlabs-privacy.
